# Notebook Guide

## 00 — Business problem and PoC design

Frames the business problem, assumptions, data entities and success metrics.

## 01 — Synthetic healthcare data generation

Creates a realistic synthetic dataset with clinics, demand, marketing activity, seasonality and operational constraints.

## 02 — EDA and forecasting validation

Explores demand behaviour and introduces rolling-origin validation.

## 03 — Statistical models

Implements naive, seasonal naive, moving average, SARIMAX and optional Prophet models.

## 04 — Global ML forecaster

Builds a lag-feature ML model that learns across all clinics.

## 05 — LSTM and TimeGPT optional models

Shows how deep-learning and foundation-model approaches can be added without making the core PoC fragile.

## 06 — Staffing optimisation

Converts forecasted visits into recommended clinicians, nurses and front-desk staff.
